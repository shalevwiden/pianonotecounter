About

Record a performance, export a real MIDI file.

Piano Session Recorder captures everything you play — over USB MIDI or straight from your computer keyboard — counts every note live, and writes a Standard MIDI File you can drop into Synthesia to generate a piano roll.

Two ways to play

Plug in a MIDI instrument such as the Yamaha P-145BT and it is detected automatically — it always plays and records. The source toggle decides whether the computer keyboard is also live, so switch to MIDI Device when you want typing to stay silent.

Accurate export

Sessions are written as SMF Format 1 at 480 PPQN with a dedicated tempo track. Held notes are closed automatically and leading silence is trimmed so the roll starts on your first note.

Built for capture

A large live counter, notes per second, peak NPS and elapsed time are all sized for screen recording. Dark and light themes are included.

Your mapping

The QWERTY layout follows the familiar Virtual Piano arrangement, and every key can be rebound from the settings panel. Preferences persist between sessions.

Keyboard layout

The home row plays white keys left to right starting at Middle C, and the row above holds the black keys in their natural gaps.

Requirements

Web MIDI needs a Chromium-based browser such as Chrome or Edge, served over localhost or HTTPS. Computer-keyboard input and audio work everywhere.
